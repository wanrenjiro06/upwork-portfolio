How many languages does Quillo support?
Quillo supports 25+ languages including English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Japanese, Korean, and Chinese. All plans have access to multilingual generation.
Is the content original and plagiarism-free?
Yes. Every piece of content generated by Quillo is 100% original, created fresh based on your input. You can also run content through any plagiarism checker for peace of mind.
Can I use the content commercially?
Absolutely. All content generated with Quillo is yours to use commercially — publish, sell, or distribute however you like. Full commercial rights included in all plans.
Does Quillo need special writing experience?
No experience needed. Quillo is designed for everyone — from complete beginners to professional copywriters. Just describe what you need and the AI handles the rest.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Of course. There are no lock-in contracts. Cancel anytime from your dashboard and you'll keep access until the end of your billing period.

Our support team is ready to help. We respond within a few hours on business days, and often much faster.
