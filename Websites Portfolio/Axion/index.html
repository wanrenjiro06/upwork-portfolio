FAQs

Answers you're looking for

Quick answers to common questions about Axion.

How does Axion automate my business?

Axion connects to your existing tools, learns your workflows, and deploys autonomous AI agents that run those workflows for you — from data entry and reporting to outreach and support. You describe the outcome in plain English; Axion handles the rest.

Is it difficult to set up?

Not at all. Most teams are live within an afternoon. There's no code required — connect your tools, describe what you want automated, and Axion builds the workflow for you.

Which tools does Axion integrate with?

Over 100 integrations including Slack, Notion, Gmail, Stripe, HubSpot, Salesforce, Linear, and more. If you use it, Axion most likely connects to it — and we add new integrations every week.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. Axion is built for everyone. You interact with it in natural language — no flowcharts, no scripting, no engineering team required.

Is my data secure?

Yes. Axion is SOC 2 Type II compliant with end-to-end encryption, granular permissions, and full audit logs. Your data is never used to train external models.
