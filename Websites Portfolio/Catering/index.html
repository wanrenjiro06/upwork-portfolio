About A Catering House
— Our Philosophy

We believe a meal is a conversation — between grower and guest, season and room, heat and hand.

Salt & Ember began as a sixteen-seat supper club above a flower market. A decade on, we are a team of thirty — chefs, stylists, sommeliers and service staff — cooking for weddings, studios, boardrooms and families across three cities.

Brief

FAQ Questions We Answer Often

Before
you ask.

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Email hello@saltandember.co — we reply within one working day.

How far in advance should we book? +

For weddings and large events, three to nine months is ideal. For private dinners and smaller gatherings, two to four weeks is usually enough. We do take on last-minute events when the calendar allows — just ask.

Do you travel? +

Yes. We’re based across London, Lisbon and New York and regularly cater destination weddings and residencies. Travel and logistics are quoted transparently inside your proposal.

Can you accommodate dietary needs? +

Always. Vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free, halal, kosher-style and allergy-specific menus are part of how we work — never an afterthought. Every menu is built around your guest list.

What’s included in the quote? +

Food, service staff, kitchen setup and clear-down are standard. Bar, wine, tableware, linen, styling and equipment hire can all be added — itemised clearly so you decide what you need.

Do you offer tastings? +

Yes — a private tasting at our studio is included for weddings and large events. For smaller bookings, tastings can be arranged for a small fee that’s credited back when you confirm.

How do payments work? +

A deposit secures your date, with the balance due two weeks before the event. We accept bank transfer and card. Everything is laid out in your written proposal before anything is signed.
